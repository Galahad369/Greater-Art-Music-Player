# Stack alignment repair — 1.22.20/code341

Status: local work in qualification, not a verified release or a claim of perfect alignment.

## Keep the three questions separate

1. Does the file matcher find a supported timing map?
2. Does Apply install that map without letting one singer continue alone?
3. Does the actual output stay aligned during play, seek, resume and loop?

Correct offsets and player-position logs do not prove coincident emitted audio. Sound matching follows shared backing; picture matching follows video changes. One track has one selected timing map. Matching an MV does not establish that its separately edited soundtrack is synchronized.

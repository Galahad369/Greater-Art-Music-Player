Many takes. One local mix.

Stack up to eight singers or recordings. Align by sound analyses shared patterns offline, preserves uncertain offsets and saves your mix. Pitch-preserving rate correction avoids constant small-drift seeks; this is structural alignment, not phase-perfect or variable-tempo time warping.

STACK / AUDIO

Audio drives the timeline

Start, seek, resume and primary-boundary loop regroup the singers. Optional video previews cannot directly pause the audio group.

BACKGROUND / FIT

Whole frames, no stretch

Choose Now-playing video + Fit to tile Stack video behind browsing pages. Crop fills the screen by default. Native multi-video decoding needs capable hardware.

AMBIENT / COLOR

From the current frame

Ambient gradients sample representative colors from the existing video surface. No extra Ambient decoder, source-quality cap or network request.
